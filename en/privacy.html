2. THE SHORT VERSION

Airplane Sudoku has no account, no login, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect personal data, and it plays completely offline.

No network request of any kind is made until you choose to watch an advertisement. That includes the consent check itself.

There is one exception, and it only happens if you ask for it: if you choose to watch an advertisement to support the app, an advertising request is sent to Google. That is described in section 5.

3. WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is saved locally and is never transmitted by us:

  • your points, ranks, streaks, best times, games played and puzzles seen
  • which technique lessons you have completed
  • your settings (language, theme, timer, highlighting, vibration, mistake limit, reminder time)
  • an unfinished game so you can resume it
  • which motivation messages have already been shown

This is removed when you uninstall the app. You can clear it at any time via Menu > Statistics > Reset all progress.

4. PERMISSIONS

  • Notifications: only if you switch reminders on. On Android 13 and newer your device asks you first. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device; no server is involved and no data is sent.
  • Internet and network state: used only to request an advertisement you have chosen to watch, and to check whether you are online before offering one.
  • Advertising ID: required by the advertising SDK. See section 5.

The app requests no other permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

5. ADVERTISING

Advertising is voluntary and never required. Nothing in the game is locked behind an advertisement and none is shown unless you tap "Yes, show me an ad".

Until that moment the advertising SDK is not started at all and no advertising request is made. If you never accept, no advertising data ever leaves your device.

When you do accept, Google acts as our advertising provider and may receive and process:

  • device and account identifiers, including the advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, and the approximate location derived from it
  • your interactions with the advertisement shown
  • diagnostic information about the advertising component, such as performance and error data

Google uses this for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. All of it is encrypted in transit. Google's own handling is described at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites https://business.safety.google/privacy/

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads, which also lets you opt out of personalised advertising at device level.

CONSENT (EEA, UK AND SWITZERLAND)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown before any advertising request is made, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Nothing is requested until you have answered it.

The form appears the first time an advertisement could be offered, not when the app starts, because nothing advertising-related happens before that point.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via Menu > Ad privacy choices. If you withdraw consent, no further advertising requests are made.

6. CHILDREN

The app is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone. It is not listed in Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

7. DATA WE HOLD

None. We operate no server, no account system and no database. We cannot identify you and have nothing to disclose, correct or delete. Requests about advertising data are a matter for Google using the links in section 5.

8. CHANGES

This policy is updated whenever the app's data handling changes, and the Google Play Data Safety declaration is updated to match.
